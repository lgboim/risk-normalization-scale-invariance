# Research chronology audit for the scale-invariance manuscript

This audit distinguishes facts preserved by contemporaneous files from timing that cannot be reconstructed independently. It does not convert retrospective choices into preregistration.

## Named period splits

The earliest preserved complete English manuscript, dated August 31, 2026, already reports the four periods `2011–2017`, `2018–2019`, `2020–2021`, and `2022–2023`, and identifies `2022–2023` minus `2011–2017` as the headline contrast. Its SHA-256 is `9da37485d37c087870cc06aa94c53dffa292097fd112a3a199199af41b3b4d7f`.

The period means had therefore been inspected before the Sierra repair protocol was frozen and before the present scale-invariance framing was written. The available archive does not establish when or by what search process those four period boundaries were first selected. They must consequently remain retrospective named summaries, not confirmatory breakpoints.

## Endpoint enumeration

The temporal-placebo protocol was frozen on August 31, 2026 before its endpoint ranks, tail probabilities, sign counts, or pre-2018 transition results were calculated. For NQ, it enumerates every ordered pairing of a seven-consecutive-year baseline and a later nonoverlapping two-consecutive-year window available from complete calendar years 2011–2025. The protocol explicitly states that the published period means and annual extension were already known and prohibits selection of a favorable endpoint. Its SHA-256 is `9228d284e1431af7878bd4c15b33be811fb72312509d2181259a4d2a7386dde0`.

The resulting endpoint audit is retrospective falsification, not prospective confirmation. The results artifact has SHA-256 `5401d995e81aed44ad1c52fa95da52b6fb83dd7cb3e2ced322a74aba6468580b`.

Under the stated four-dollar convention, the NQ headline contrast ranks 12th of 28 equal-geometry transitions and third of seven comparisons that hold the 2011–2017 baseline fixed. All seven fixed-baseline comparisons are positive. The selected 2022–2023 endpoint is therefore neither locally maximal nor uniquely responsible for the sign, while the overlapping enumeration remains descriptive rather than calibrated inference. This frozen endpoint audit was NQ-only; it does not support a corresponding ES rank claim.
